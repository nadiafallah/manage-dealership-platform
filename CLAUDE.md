Read these first, in this order:

1. [docs/PRODUCT_SPEC_FA.md](docs/PRODUCT_SPEC_FA.md) — the approved product specification. It is the **single source of truth**: wherever code, README, PROGRESS.md or any other document disagrees with it, the specification wins.

3. [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) — current state and the exact next step.

Approved visual mockups: [docs/design/mockups/](docs/design/mockups/) (Design Component HTML; read for structure and CSS, they are not meant to run).
